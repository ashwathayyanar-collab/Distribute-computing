How to read this

Each row is one process running independently. Time moves left to right. A dot marks an event; the number above it is that process's Lamport clock immediately after the event. A diagonal line connects a send to its matching receive — notice the receiving clock always jumps to at least one more than the value carried by the message, per Lamport's second rule.
